# Data Center Types

## By ownership and operation

- **Enterprise** — These are built and run by a single company for its own workloads. They're usually on the premises.
- **Colocation** — Some provider rents the space, power, and cooling. You simply supply the servers.
- **Hyperscale** — These are huge buildings that are operated by cloud and big companies. They use a lot of power.
- **Managed or hosted** — These are centers where the provider owns the hardware and leases it to regular people.

## By location and size

- **Edge** — These are small sites that are placed close to users to cut latency. They're normally near cell towers and retail locations.
- **Regional** — The are large centralized centers that handle the heavy lifting.

## By workload

- **General compute & storage** — These are your traditional racks with only a moderate power use.
- **AI or HPC** — These are ultra-dense GPU clusters. They use really high power per rack.

## By tier

| Tier | Characteristic |
|------|----------------|
| I | Single non-redundant distribution path |
| II | Redundant capacity components |
| III | Concurrently maintainable |
| IV | Fault tolerant |

## Misc types

- **Containerized** — These are like small premade units that are dropped on site.
- **Carrier hotels** — These are buildings where heavy networks meet.
